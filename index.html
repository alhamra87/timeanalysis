What is the Gartner TIME model?

TIME is a framework from Gartner for application portfolio rationalisation. Every application is rated on two dimensions and the result places it into one of four actions:

MigrateHigh value · Low tech fit
InvestHigh value · High tech fit
EliminateLow value · Low tech fit
TolerateLow value · High tech fit
  • Business value (vertical axis) — how much the business needs and benefits from the application.
  • Technical fit (horizontal axis) — how healthy, secure, supportable and future-proof the technology is.

An analogy: clearing out your wardrobe

Imagine sorting every item of clothing you own by two questions: "Do I actually wear it?" (business value) and "What condition is it in?" (technical fit).

  • Invest — your favourite jacket in great condition. Wear it, care for it, buy things that go with it.
  • Migrate — your favourite shoes, but the soles are worn through. You need them, so get them re-soled or buy a modern replacement.
  • Tolerate — a perfectly good shirt you rarely wear. It's not hurting anyone; keep it, but don't spend money on it.
  • Eliminate — a torn sweater you haven't worn in years. It takes up space; donate or bin it.

Applications work the same way — except the "wardrobe space" is budget, licences, infrastructure and support effort.

How to run an assessment with this tool

  1. Build the inventory on the Applications tab (add manually, import CSV, or start from the sample).
  2. Agree the criteria and weights on the Criteria tab with business and IT stakeholders.
  3. Score each application 1–5 per criterion on the Assess tab. Ideally, business owners score value and architects score technical fit.
  4. Review the matrix on the Dashboard. Challenge results near the threshold lines, and use an override (with a documented rationale) where context outweighs the numbers.
  5. Turn results into a roadmap: funding for Invest, modernisation projects for Migrate, cost caps for Tolerate and retirement plans for Eliminate.

Scoring scale

ScoreMeaning

Each dimension's score is the weighted average of its answered criteria. Unanswered criteria are ignored, and the completeness meter shows how much of the assessment is done.
